Case Studies

A few problems I noticed, and what I built to close them.

Stakeholder Ticketing Portal & Department SOP

Process design · Stakeholder communication

Problem

No consistent way for stakeholders to contact the department. Requests went untracked, and different people received different answers to the same question.

Approach

Designed a ticketing portal with status tracking, filtering, and escalation, then authored the department's first Standard Operating Procedure — delivered as an interactive PDF and stored centrally.

Outcome

Requests now follow a traceable lifecycle from submission to resolution. The SOP is used daily by new staff and managers as the default reference.

Copyright & Privacy Awareness Module

Instructional design · Security-adjacent training

Problem

The sudden shift to online lesson delivery during COVID-19 introduced new risks — instructors screen-sharing licensed materials and exposing their home environments on camera — that most had never had to consider.

Approach

Authored the script, slides, and assessment from source material, then used Adobe InDesign's digital publishing features to build a richer, more interactive self-study interface than the LMS could offer alone.

Outcome

A concrete, actionable awareness module that made an abstract risk tangible for a non-technical audience — deployed org-wide via SCORM.

Access Control & Security Audit

Governance · Risk reduction

Problem

SharePoint access was granted to individuals one by one — difficult to audit, and easy to lose track of as people changed roles.

Approach

Learned SharePoint security groups from the ground up, applied them across departmental teams and sites, and made role-based, group-level access the mandatory standard.

Outcome

Auditable, consistent access control across all team data — closing a gap nobody had asked me to look at.
